## Consequential assumptions

- **Cost of Capital / WACC (8.90%):** Because Marriott is a mature cash generator, over 70% of discounted enterprise value resides in the terminal value. A 100 bps shift in WACC will swing valuation significantly.
- **System Unit Growth (4.5%–5.0% Net Rooms) & RevPAR (3.0%–3.5%):** System room expansion drives the recurring franchise fee pool independent of short-term economic cycles.
- **Asset-Light Capex Discipline (<$600M corporate tech):** The thesis relies on third-party hotel owners funding building renovations; if Marriott must invest significant capital into owned properties, free cash flow conversion will deteriorate.

## Unknowns

- **International Demand Stability:** Whether uneven international RevPAR performance (such as Middle East headwinds noted in Q2 2026) represents a temporary regional issue or a broader deceleration.
- **Co-Branded Credit Card Royalty Trajectory:** The exact growth rate and renewal economics of the Bonvoy credit card agreements with JPMorgan Chase and American Express.
- **Leverage & Buyback Headroom:** How long Marriott can sustain \$2.5B+ in annual share buybacks while carrying \$16.2B in senior debt and negative book equity (-\$3.8B).
